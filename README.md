🚀 STAR STRIKER 1945: Official Flight Manual & Tactical Guide

Welcome to the cockpit, Pilot. The fate of the galaxy rests on your wings. This manual provides the vital flight coordinates, control layouts, and tactical intelligence needed to survive humanity's ultimate aerial showdown.

🌌 1. Mission Briefing

The galaxy is under siege by relentless waves of alien vanguard units. As the commander of the state-of-the-art Star Striker fighter jet, your objective is to breach enemy airspace, neutralize hostile fighters, and secure the highest possible score before your hull integrity fails.

🎮 2. Control Configurations

Star Striker 1945 supports fully integrated cross-platform controls for both desktop and mobile devices.

💻 PC Controls (Keyboard)

Movement: Use the Arrow Keys or W / A / S / D keys to maneuver your fighter in all directions.

Primary Fire: Automatic. Your dual laser cannons fire continuously to allow focused navigation.

Tactical Shockwave (Skill): Press [ X ] to unleash the ultimate shockwave.

Quick Restart: Press [ R ] on the Game Over screen to quickly restart your last selected mission.

📱 Mobile Controls (Touch Screen)

Movement: Touch and drag anywhere on the screen. The fighter will smoothly track your finger's relative movement.

Tactical Shockwave (Skill): Tap the dedicated floating [ SKILL ] button located at the bottom right corner of your screen.

🛰️ 4. Advanced Game Mechanics

🌀 Dynamic Difficulty Ramp

The enemy fleet adapts to your performance. As your combat score increases, the speed of enemy vessels and bullet velocity scale exponentially.

The real-time Difficulty Multiplier ($M$) is calculated dynamically using the following formula:

$$M = B + \lfloor \frac{\text{Score}}{500} \rfloor \times 0.18$$

(Where $B$ is your selected mode's Base Speed Multiplier, and $\lfloor \dots \rfloor$ represents the floor function).

❤️ Recovery Items (Heal Packs)

Destroying enemy vessels has a $15\%$ chance to trigger a Nano-Repair Kit (Green Box) drop.

Collecting a kit restores $+25\ \text{HP}$ to your hull.

Maximum hull integrity is capped at $100\ \text{HP}$.

⚡ Defensive Shockwave Aura

When overwhelmed by enemy plasma bullet fields, deploy your Shockwave skill.

Radius Expansion: Expands outwards to a maximum radius of $370\ \text{pixels}$.

Defensive Cleansing: Instantly vaporizes all enemy projectiles inside its radius.

Offensive Destruction: Deals fatal damage to all alien fighters caught within the blast wave, granting you immediate destruction points.

🔊 Retro Audio Synthesis

Powered by advanced Web Audio API technology, the game synthesizes real-time retro arcade sound effects directly within your browser (no external files required):

High-frequency pulse lasers upon firing.

Low-frequency white noise explosions when hostile fleets are crushed.

Alarm triggers during hull impact.

🏆 5. Tactical Pro-Tips for High Scores

Prioritize Item Collection: Do not let Green Heal Packs slip past the bottom of the screen. Managing your $HP$ is crucial as the difficulty ramps up.

Conserve Shockwaves: Save your shockwaves for Hard mode bullet patterns or when you are trapped in a corner. Using a shockwave can clear the entire screen and save you from losing $28\ \text{HP}$ from a direct ship collision.

Control the Center: Staying near the center-bottom of the screen gives you the best tactical viewing angle to dodge incoming plasma spheres and scoop up falling salvage kits.
